Phase 4 — Script production
3 formulas (70M views combined)
Reframe — 24M views

Find what they feel guilty about. Don't tell them to stop. Tell them to redirect it. "If you're going to overthink… overthink the positive."

You're doing it wrong — 23M views

Pick the most boring daily habit. Make it feel dangerous. "You drink water the wrong way, my friend."

Validation — 22.4M views

Don't teach. Say what they already feel but can't articulate. "Keep it private until it's permanent." You're the mirror, not the teacher.

5 content rules (Pounds)

Relatability — "that's me" moment within 3 seconds

Specificity — vague scripts die; use exact numbers and names

Open loops — tease what's coming so they can't leave mid-video

Emotional contrast — move between at least two emotional states

Rewatch value — end with something people save or share

Batch 30–100 scripts before posting anything. Use Claude with character + audience + pain point + examples of scripts that worked.
Phase 5 — Optimise and scale
Data diagnostics
At 3 seconds — TSR

Drop-off >60%: hook is the failure. Not the product, not the persona.

At 25% — VTR

High drop-off: persona-audience mismatch or pacing. Not the hook.

Winners

Don't tweak. Duplicate 10–30× with minimal variation. Capitalise on algorithmic momentum before it fades.
